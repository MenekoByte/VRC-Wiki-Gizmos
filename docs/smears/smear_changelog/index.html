Changelog

v6​

Smear Generator​

  • Plain Mesh Renderers are now full smear sources allowing props and weapons to get smears too.
  • Combine Meshes was removed. Each group becomes one smear, and meshes in Keep Separate get their own. The Combine Mesh Groups header is gone and the lists have a short hint.
  • Generate is blocked with a "These need fixing:" popup while anything needs attention. Missing or broken things are highlighted in red.
  • A warning appears when a source mesh doesn't have Read/Write enabled.
  • Section headers show a pending mark (* and an edge bar) when something in them differs from the installed smear: yellow for Update, orange for Regenerate.
  • Update Smear and Regenerate Smear keep a faint tint at all times and light up fully when needed.
  • Fixed Regenerate staying highlighted when nothing had changed.
  • Fixed the Meshes section always showing as changed.
  • The preview picks up Update Smear and Apply immediately. The preview window has a fixed height now.
  • Generation info (source meshes, settings, triangle counts) is saved into the generated mesh asset.
  • "No generated smear detected" replaces the Detected Smear field.
  • Help buttons on each section open the matching wiki page, and there is an Open Wiki button next to Re-read Settings.
  • Added a credits bar at the bottom links to the author and the store page.
  • The window's minimum width now fits the cost row.

MeshWorks​

  • The generator now runs on MeshWorks, a shared mesh and armature library shipped as a DLL. Bone merging, auto exclude and merge, bone paths, mesh combining, hull building helpers and the mesh editing toolkit moved there.
  • The generated smear renders exactly the same as before: every triangle matches the old output in all 24 configurations tested.
  • The old bundled simplifier was replaced by a new decimator that can reduce meshes further while keeping UV seams. It produces the same result as before its speed-up, now about 30% faster (around 1.5 s for a full body mesh in Unity).
  • Combined meshes keep correct normals when mirrored, and bone paths with special characters are handled.

Smear Material​

  • A Presets window lists the presets with thumbnails. Pick one and use Create Material or Apply to Current Material.
  • Create and Apply sit next to the Smear Material field. Create overwrites an existing material in place and puts it on the installed smear.
  • Apply only swaps the material.
  • New Update Material button sets Curve Trail, slot textures and Hide Distance. It glows yellow when the material is out of date.

Smear Mesh​

  • Advanced settings have new Read/Write (on by default) and Skip Vertex Colors (off by default) options.
  • Advanced toggles now have the checkbox first.
  • The % sign sits after the Mesh Density field.

Bone Manager​

  • Armatures are grouped under the object they belong to.
  • Link Selection was replaced with a Presets dropdown that saves and loads bone setups as JSON files. Load replaces the current setup.
  • Exclude Unweighted Bones and Merge Small Bones now add to the current setup instead of replacing it.
  • Merge Small Bones merges intermediate finger bones into their parent and excludes their children, including bones linked to them.
  • Switching avatars clears the old Root Bone, exclusions and merges.

Smear Shader​

  • Ghost Gradient colors are baked now. Cuts ghosts performance cost by about 4x vs no ghosts.
  • Ghost colors are only worked out for the colour mode in use. Before, the gradient ran for every pixel even in Rainbow mode.
  • Per-strip maths (ghost spacing, band scale, ink shift and stretch) is worked out once per vertex instead of once per pixel, about 10% faster on a real smear with the same look.
  • Fully transparent pixels are dropped early in normal and premultiplied blend modes.
  • Removed Geometric ghost mode. Standard and Sorted remain. Geometric materials switch to Sorted when unlocked.
  • Section help buttons were added to the headers.
